⚠️ Reality Check: Profit vs. Revenue
$50-80K MRR revenue ≠ profit. Here's the breakdown at Phase 4:
  • $60K MRR example: $600K annual revenue → ~$200-250K profit (after team payroll & ops)
  • This is yours to keep OR reinvest in growth
  • To hit $1M by 20, you need to scale beyond $60K MRR, sell the company, or run this path 2-3x over
🎯 Path to $1M by Age 20

Three Viable Routes (Choose One or Combine):


Route A: Grow Revenue Beyond Phase 4
  • Hit $100K+ MRR by month 20 (more aggressive hiring + marketing)
  • Keep 40%+ as profit = $40K+/month = $480K+/year
  • Possible but requires perfect execution

Route B: Sell the Business
  • Agency with $60K MRR + strong team typically valued at $500K-$1M (3-5x revenue multiple)
  • If you hit $800K/year revenue by month 20, could sell for $1-2M
  • Best path to $1M cash

Route C: Diversify Income
  • Keep agency as cash cow ($40-50K/month profit)
  • Launch productized service, SaaS, or digital product ($20-50K/month)
  • Combine for $60-100K/month total
✅ Critical Success Factors
  • Client Retention: Focus on keeping clients happy (recurring revenue is your cash engine)
  • Margin Protection: Don't race to the bottom on pricing; raise rates aggressively
  • Hiring Right: First 1-2 hires make or break your growth (hire slowly, fire fast)
  • Delegation: Your job changes from "doing" to "leading" — embrace this early
  • Systems: Document everything; if the team can't run it without you, you own a job, not a business
  • Cash Management: Profit ≠ cash. Track cash flow weekly (this kills fast-growing businesses)
